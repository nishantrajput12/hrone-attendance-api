## Looked at and decided it was fine

- `load_dotenv()` without override: a real environment variable still beats the `.env` file, which is what the assignment wants.
- The endpoints are plain `def`, so FastAPI runs them in a thread pool, and `MongoClient` is thread-safe. The races come from check-then-insert, not from threading.
- `datetime.fromisoformat("YYYY-MM-DD")` works on Python 3.11.
- `int(total_seconds() / 60)` is an exact floor for whole non-negative seconds, and `max(0, ...)` stops negative overtime. The only problem on that line (47) was the missing 30-minute rule; the other `compute_overtime` defects are on line 46 (rows 2 and 3).
- Dates kept as `YYYY-MM-DD` strings and compared as strings is correct, because zero-padded dates sort in calendar order.
- The `{"_id": 0}` projection and `doc.pop("_id")` hide `_id` correctly.
- `IST = timezone(timedelta(hours=5, minutes=30))` is correct; India has no daylight saving. The bugs were in where it was used.
- Pydantic ignores unknown body keys, so a client cannot send `late_minutes` or `work_hours`, which matches the contract.
